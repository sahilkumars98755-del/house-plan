# Proposed Residence — Ground + 1 with Open Terrace

Concept floor plans for **Smt. Pritee Sah / Sri Jeetendra Kumar Sah** —
Daheriya, Ward 44, Dist. Katihar (Bihar).

## The plot
- Irregular plot, roughly **15 ft road frontage x ~63 ft deep**; area **2.1 decimal (~914 sq ft)**
- Fronts a **12'-0" wide road**
- Khata 185 · Khesra 86 · Thana 98 · Toji 1172

## The brief
- Ground + 1 with an **open terrace** on top
- **4 bedrooms** — master + two children's rooms + a guest room
- Children's bedrooms on the **ground floor**
- **No attached bathrooms** — one common toilet + bath per floor (Indian WC)
- Pooja, study, closed (modular) kitchen, dining, living with sofa + TV unit
- Parking for **1 car + 1 scooter**
- Open terrace with a parapet wall and an overhead water tank
- Vastu followed loosely

## Layout

### Ground floor (~648 sq ft)
Entry porch + pooja → living / drawing → study → child's bedroom 1 →
child's bedroom 2 → common toilet (Indian WC) + bath with washing machine →
staircase. Parking (1 car + 1 scooter) sits in the front setback.

### First floor (~648 sq ft)
Balcony (front) → master bedroom (queen bed, wardrobe, dressing table) →
modular kitchen → dining → guest bedroom → common toilet + bath → staircase.

### Terrace (~648 sq ft)
Open, with a 3'-6" parapet wall, overhead water tank and a stair mumty.
